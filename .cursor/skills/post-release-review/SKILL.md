# Post-Release Review

Playbook steps **S7.2, S7.3** (`devops`). Paths, gates, and routes come from your step card.

## When to use
- The last release point's observation window has ended, or a major incident tied to a release occurred.

## Input
- Observation summaries, incidents, defects, rollback history, the success measures of the PRD and of the released features' specs.

## Process
1. Compare outcomes with the success measures.
2. List what worked and what failed, with evidence.
3. Propose follow-ups — fix, debt, or requirement change — each with an owner.
4. Recommend closing the cycle or authorizing corrective work.

## Output
- Post-release review in the shape of the `POST-RELEASE-REVIEW.md` template.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] Every follow-up has an owner
- [ ] The recommendation is explicit
