## Visual identity

Audience: SMB owners and executives deciding whether a founder-led AI consultancy is credible. Tone: calm, confident, plain-spoken; low density with generous whitespace. Brand attributes: Clear, Grounded, Considered. Signature: typography-led editorial layout; Services as a ruled problem/outcome list; Approach as a numbered 1–4 sequence. Imagery: type-led, no stock or AI clichés; Lucide-style outline icons only where they carry content. Motion: opacity and transform 150–250ms; disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
